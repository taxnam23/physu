# `physu` 0.6.0 (Unreleased)

## Added
- Created `physu.waves` subpackage scaffold (`src/physu/waves/__init__.py` and internal `_helpers.py`) and bumped package version to `0.6.0`.
- **`oscillations`**: Spring-mass and pendulum oscillators (`spring_mass_angular_frequency`, `spring_mass_period`, `simple_pendulum_period`, `physical_pendulum_period`) and SHM kinematics/energy (`shm_displacement`, `shm_velocity`, `shm_acceleration`, `shm_max_speed`, `shm_max_acceleration`, `shm_energy`, `shm_speed_at_position`).
- **`mechanical`**: Wave kinematics (`wave_speed`, `wavelength`, `wave_frequency`, `wavenumber`, `wave_phase_speed`), taut string dynamics (`string_wave_speed`, `string_wave_power`), and intensity (`wave_intensity`, `spherical_wave_intensity`).
- **`standing`**: Fixed-string and pipe harmonics (`string_harmonic_frequency`, `open_pipe_harmonic_frequency`, `closed_pipe_harmonic_frequency`, `string_harmonic_wavelength`, `closed_pipe_harmonic_wavelength`), `fundamental_tension_for_frequency`, and `beat_frequency`.
- **`acoustics`**: Ideal-gas sound speed (`speed_of_sound_ideal_gas`), logarithmic sound levels (`sound_intensity_level`, `intensity_from_sound_level`, `sound_pressure_level`), `acoustic_impedance`, and `acoustic_reflection_coefficient`.
- **`doppler`**: Classical acoustic shift (`doppler_frequency`), longitudinal relativistic shift (`relativistic_doppler_frequency`), `mach_number`, and `mach_angle`.

### Examples & Tests
- Added real-world waves and acoustics script [waves.py](../examples/waves.py) and updated `README.md`.
- Added unit tests in `tests/test_waves.py` covering all five submodules, decibel/neper handling, external Pint registries, and validation errors.
