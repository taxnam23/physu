# `physu` 0.3.0 (2026-11-17)

Adds mechanical work, energy, conservation, and power (`physu.energy`), plus real-world runnable examples across every domain.

## Added

### Energy (`physu.energy`)
- **`work`**: Constant-force work (`mechanical_work`), work-energy theorem (`net_work`), `gravitational_work`, and `elastic_work`.
- **`kinetic`**: Translational kinetic energy (`kinetic_energy`, `kinetic_energy_from_momentum`, `speed_from_kinetic_energy`, `momentum_from_kinetic_energy`).
- **`potential`**: Near-surface and universal gravitational potential energy (`gravitational_potential_energy`, `universal_gravitational_potential_energy`) and `elastic_potential_energy`.
- **`conservation`**: Total mechanical energy (`mechanical_energy`), `non_conservative_work`, conservation solvers (`final_speed_conservation`, `final_height_conservation`), `escape_speed`, and `mechanical_efficiency`.
- **`power`**: Mechanical power (`average_power`, `instantaneous_power`, `work_from_power`, `force_from_power`, `velocity_from_power`).

### Examples & Tests
- Added real-world energy/power sizing script [energy_work_and_power.py](../examples/energy_work_and_power.py).
- Added unit tests in `tests/test_energy.py` (50 tests total across the suite), verified with `ruff` and strict `mypy`.

## Changed
- Replaced dry per-function prints across `examples/` with realistic real-world engineering and everyday physics scenarios, and updated `README.md`.
