# `physu` 0.7.0 (Unreleased)

## Added
- Created `physu.optics` subpackage scaffold (`src/physu/optics/__init__.py` and internal `_helpers.py`) and bumped package version to `0.7.0`.
- **`refraction`**: Optical media relations (`speed_in_medium`, `wavelength_in_medium`, `refractive_index`), `snell_refraction_angle`, `critical_angle`, and `brewster_angle`.
- **`lenses`**: Thin lens/mirror solvers (`thin_lens_image_distance`, `thin_lens_focal_length`, `lateral_magnification`), `optical_power`, `combined_optical_power`, `lensmaker_focal_length`, and `mirror_focal_length`.
- **`interference`**: Double-slit formulas (`double_slit_angle`, `double_slit_fringe_position`, `double_slit_fringe_spacing`), `thin_film_constructive_thickness`, and `michelson_displacement`.
- **`diffraction`**: Single-slit and grating diffraction (`single_slit_minimum_angle`, `diffraction_grating_angle`, `grating_spacing_from_density`), `rayleigh_angular_resolution`, and `bragg_diffraction_angle`.
