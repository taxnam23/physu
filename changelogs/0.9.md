# `physu` 0.9.0 (Unreleased)

## Added
- Created `physu.modern` subpackage scaffold (`src/physu/modern/__init__.py` and internal `_helpers.py`), added `BOHR_RADIUS`, `RYDBERG_CONSTANT`, and `WIEN_DISPLACEMENT_CONSTANT` to `physu.constants`, and bumped package version to `0.9.0`.
- **`relativity`**: Lorentz factor (`lorentz_factor`), `time_dilation`, `length_contraction`, `relativistic_velocity_addition`, `rest_energy`, `relativistic_total_energy`, `relativistic_kinetic_energy`, `relativistic_momentum`, and `energy_momentum_relation`.
- **`photons`**: Photon solvers (`photon_energy`, `photon_momentum`), photoelectric effect (`photoelectric_max_kinetic_energy`, `photoelectric_threshold_frequency`, `photoelectric_stopping_potential`), `compton_wavelength_shift`, and `wien_peak_wavelength`.
- **`quantum`**: Matter waves (`de_broglie_wavelength`, `de_broglie_wavelength_particle`), Heisenberg lower bounds (`heisenberg_min_momentum_uncertainty`, `heisenberg_min_energy_uncertainty`), and `infinite_well_energy`.
- **`atomic`**: Bohr model (`bohr_orbit_radius`, `bohr_energy_level`, `bohr_transition_energy`) and `rydberg_wavelength`.
