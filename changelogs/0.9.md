# `physu` 0.9.0 (2026-11-23)

Adds modern physics (`physu.modern`) across five submodules — special relativity, photons, quantum mechanics, Bohr atomic spectra, and nuclear/radioactive decay — plus derived CODATA constants and real-world examples.

## Added

### Modern Physics (`physu.modern`)
- **`relativity`**: Lorentz factor (`lorentz_factor`), `time_dilation`, `length_contraction`, `relativistic_velocity_addition`, `rest_energy`, `relativistic_total_energy`, `relativistic_kinetic_energy`, `relativistic_momentum`, and `energy_momentum_relation`.
- **`photons`**: Photon solvers (`photon_energy`, `photon_momentum`), photoelectric effect (`photoelectric_max_kinetic_energy`, `photoelectric_threshold_frequency`, `photoelectric_stopping_potential`), `compton_wavelength_shift`, and `wien_peak_wavelength`.
- **`quantum`**: Matter waves (`de_broglie_wavelength`, `de_broglie_wavelength_particle`), Heisenberg lower bounds (`heisenberg_min_momentum_uncertainty`, `heisenberg_min_energy_uncertainty`), and `infinite_well_energy`.
- **`atomic`**: Bohr model (`bohr_orbit_radius`, `bohr_energy_level`, `bohr_transition_energy`) and `rydberg_wavelength`.
- **`nuclear`**: `mass_energy_equivalence`, decay rate solvers (`decay_constant`, `half_life_from_decay_constant`), `radioactive_remaining`, `radioactive_activity` (`Bq` / `Ci`), and `radiometric_age`.

### Constants, Examples & Tests
- Added `BOHR_RADIUS`, `RYDBERG_CONSTANT`, and `WIEN_DISPLACEMENT_CONSTANT` to `physu.constants`.
- Added real-world modern physics script [modern.py](../examples/modern.py) and updated `README.md`.
- Added unit tests in `tests/test_modern.py` and `tests/test_constants.py` covering all five submodules, SI/atomic/nuclear conversions (`eV`, `keV`, `MeV`, `GeV`, `u`, `amu`, `angstrom`, `fm`, `Bq`, `Ci`, `year`), external Pint registries, and validation errors (124 tests total across the suite), verified with `ruff` and strict `mypy`.
