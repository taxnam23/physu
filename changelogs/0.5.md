# `physu` 0.5.0 (Unreleased)

## Added
- Created `physu.electromagnetism` subpackage scaffold (`src/physu/electromagnetism/__init__.py` and internal `_helpers.py`), added `COULOMB_CONSTANT` to `physu.constants`, and bumped package version to `0.5.0`.
- **`electrostatics`**: Coulomb's law (`coulomb_force`), electric fields and forces (`electric_field_point_charge`, `electric_force`, `uniform_electric_field`), electric potential, energy, and work (`electric_potential_point_charge`, `electric_potential_energy`, `electric_work`), and electric flux / Gauss's law (`electric_flux`, `gauss_electric_flux`).
- **`circuits`**: Ohm's law solvers (`ohms_law_voltage`, `ohms_law_current`, `ohms_law_resistance`), `wire_resistance`, multi-form `electric_power`, `joule_heating`, `series_resistance`, `parallel_resistance`, and `voltage_divider`.
- **`capacitance`**: Capacitor solvers (`capacitance`, `capacitor_charge`, `capacitor_voltage`), `parallel_plate_capacitance`, multi-form `capacitor_energy`, `series_capacitance`, `parallel_capacitance`, `rc_time_constant`, `rc_charging_voltage`, and `rc_discharging_voltage`.
- **`magnetism`**: Lorentz forces (`lorentz_magnetic_force`, `magnetic_force_on_wire`), cyclotron motion (`cyclotron_radius`, `cyclotron_frequency`), magnetic fields (`magnetic_field_wire`, `magnetic_field_loop_center`, `magnetic_field_solenoid`), and `force_per_length_parallel_wires`.
