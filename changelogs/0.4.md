# `physu` 0.4.0 (2026-11-17)

Adds thermodynamics (`physu.thermodynamics`) with safe handling of Pint offset temperature units, plus `STEFAN_BOLTZMANN_CONSTANT` and real-world examples.

## Added

### Thermodynamics (`physu.thermodynamics`)
- **`temperature`**: Absolute-zero-validated temperature conversions (`to_kelvin`, `to_celsius`, `to_fahrenheit`), offset-safe `temperature_difference`, and thermal expansion (`linear_thermal_expansion`, `volumetric_thermal_expansion`).
- **`calorimetry`**: Sensible heat (`sensible_heat`), phase-change `latent_heat`, `specific_heat_capacity`, and multi-body `equilibrium_temperature`.
- **`ideal_gas`**: Ideal gas law solvers (`ideal_gas_pressure`, `ideal_gas_volume`, `ideal_gas_moles`, `ideal_gas_temperature`), `combined_gas_law`, `rms_molecular_speed`, and `average_molecular_kinetic_energy`.
- **`laws`**: First law (`internal_energy_change`), `isobaric_work`, `isothermal_work`, `thermal_efficiency`, `carnot_efficiency`, and `isothermal_entropy_change`.
- **`heat_transfer`**: Fourier conduction (`conduction_heat_rate`) and Stefan-Boltzmann thermal radiation (`radiation_power`).

### Constants, Examples & Tests
- Added `STEFAN_BOLTZMANN_CONSTANT` to `physu.constants`.
- Added real-world thermodynamics script [thermodynamics.py](../examples/thermodynamics.py) and updated `README.md`.
- Added unit tests in `tests/test_thermodynamics.py` and `tests/test_constants.py` (61 tests total across the suite), verified with `ruff` and strict `mypy`.
